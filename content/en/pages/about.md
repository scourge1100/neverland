# Hi, I'm a developer who solves problems and writes them down.

## How I Work

I don't stop at building a feature. I check that it works well in the real environment where it is used. When something goes wrong, I follow the evidence to find whether the cause is in the code, the data, the configuration, or the operating environment, and then fix it.

## Why I Write Things Down

A finished result alone makes it hard to reuse the decisions made along the way. I record the background of the problem, the approach I chose, the trial and error, and what I learned, so they become the starting point for the next piece of work.

## What I'm Interested In

- Products and tools that make complicated work simple
- Automation that reduces repetitive work
- Narrowing the gap between development and real operations
- Documentation and knowledge systems that last

Neverland collects technical notes from my work, case studies of representative projects, and thoughts I want to come back to.
